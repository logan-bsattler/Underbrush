# Zone rules: one rule of its own for each zone

Rank 8 on the Phase 6 list (`review-plan.md`), size L. Drafted 2026-10-30 for Ben's approval. Nothing is built.

## The answer

Each of the six zones gets one rule of its own. Each rule fits in one line and changes either what you know about
the patch or what one hazard does. None adds a hazard or makes a bust cost more.

| Zone | Rule | In one line | What it does to the walk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Meadow** | The fairy ring | A trip's first patch has a mushroom at its centre, with a ring on its back you can see | The first tap is safe, and its number shows you four cards |
| **Mixed Woods** | The mycelium | Turn a mushroom, and any mushroom beside it shows through the leaves | Turning finds leads to more finds, and shows cards that are safe to turn |
| **Old-growth** | The quiet | It's so quiet you hear further: numbers count the diagonal cards too | The same risk, with the information in a different shape |
| **Burn Scar** | Open sky | There's no canopy, so Ash is dealt face up, numbers showing | The most dangerous zone starts each patch with clues |
| **Riverbank** | The creek | The Riverbank is closed in a month of Rain. The month after, the first patch holds a rare find washed up | A month lost, a find gained |
| **The Hollow** | Turned around | Lost Trail doesn't count toward a bust. It turns you around, and you walk on to the next patch or head home | Fewer busts, more cards left unturned |

Rules apply on the surface only. The layers below keep the plain rules, just as they keep the plain look in every
season.

## Why

- **Zones today differ only by deck.** Each zone plays the same small game with different odds. One rule each
  gives each zone a way to play and a reason to pick it beyond what it deals.
- **Each rule works on a different part of the walk.** The fairy ring changes the deal, the mycelium and open sky
  change what you can see, the quiet changes how numbers count, the creek ties a zone to the weather, and turned
  around changes what a hazard does. No two feel like the same rule twice.
- **The rules arrive one at a time.** The Meadow and Mixed Woods are open from the start; each other zone opens
  with its gear. So a player meets a new rule when they open a new zone, never several at once.
- **Mild on purpose.** Danger is flavor in this game. Four rules make a walk a little safer, one is neutral and one
  trades a closed month for a find. Nothing here makes foraging harsher.
- **The early rules teach.** The fairy ring points a new player at their first tap. The mycelium rewards reading
  the patch, which is the habit the numbers ask for.

## How it plays

### The Meadow: the fairy ring

- On every surface trip, the first patch's centre card is a mushroom. It's pulled from the Meadow's draw pile at
  random, so the deck still holds the same cards.
- Face down, the card shows a faint ring of tiny caps around its leaf. Turned, it's an ordinary find.
- The centre card has four neighbours, so turning it first shows a number that covers almost half the patch.
- If the draw pile has no mushroom left (a deck mostly walked through), the patch deals as usual. A first patch
  always has one in practice.
- **Picker line:** "The fairy ring: your first patch has a mushroom at its centre."
- **Tie-in:** the Fairy Ring Champignon is already one of the Meadow's deep finds.

### The Mixed Woods: the mycelium

- When you turn a mushroom card, each face-down mushroom card beside it (orthogonally, as the numbers count)
  shows a small pale cap through the leaves on its back.
- This applies to any mushroom card, including a wild one out of season and a rare find. A rare find shows as a
  mushroom, not as rare.
- A marked card is safe to turn. Turning it may mark more, so a cluster can be followed across the patch.
- Pale threads run from the turned card to each card it marks, drawn once and then fading to a thin line.
- **Picker line:** "The mycelium: a mushroom shows you the mushrooms beside it."

### The Old-growth: the quiet

- A turned card's number counts all eight cards around it, diagonals included, instead of four.
- The centre card's number counts the whole patch. A corner card's number covers three cards, and an edge card's
  covers five.
- The number is drawn in a small ring in this zone, so it doesn't read as the usual count.
- **Picker line:** "The quiet: numbers count the diagonal cards too."

### The Burn Scar: open sky

- Ash cards are dealt face up, their numbers showing. On average, about two cards in a nine-card patch are Ash.
- They count as turned: they aren't added to the trip log and add no find, but "Nothing Left Unturned" counts
  them as seen.
- **Picker line:** "Open sky: the Ash lies face up."
- Only Ash: Fireweed and the Charred Snag are dealt face down as usual.

### The Riverbank: the creek

- In a month of Rain the creek is up and the Riverbank is closed: "Closed by high water". Rain already waters
  every bed and grows them a stage, so it's a month for the farm.
- The month after Rain, the first trip to the Riverbank finds a rare find washed up in its first patch. It's the
  usual deep find for the season, and the message says so: "The creek has left something: one card on this patch
  is a rare find."
- Only the first trip that month gets it, and only if the Riverbank is open (spring to fall).
- **Picker line in the wash month:** "Washed up after the rain: a rare find in your first patch."
  The rest of the year: "The creek: closed when it rains, and generous after."
- The weather panel's Rain rule gains "The Riverbank floods."

### The Hollow: turned around

- On the Hollow's surface, turning Lost Trail doesn't add to the hazard meter and doesn't use the Knife. It ends
  this patch: the remaining cards dim and can't be turned. You swipe on to the next patch, or head home.
- If it happens on the zone's last patch, the trip can only head home. Finds are kept, as when you head home.
- Lost Trail is still a hazard for the numbers: you still hear it.
- The message: "Turned around. The trail loops back on itself; walk on or head home."
- Cards left unturned count as passed by, so a turnaround rules out "Nothing Left Unturned" for that trip.
- **Picker line:** "Turned around: Lost Trail sends you on, not home."
- The Hollow keeps its dig rule and the founder's cache. Neither depends on hazards.

### Teaching the rules

- The rule line sits on each zone card in the picker, under the blurb and above "Deep in, this spring".
- When a rule first fires in a save, the floor message names it, for example "The fairy ring: a mushroom grows at
  its centre." After that, only the cards show it.
- DESIGN.md gets a "Zone rules" section.

## Open choices for Ben

1. **Which zones.** Recommended: **all six**. The alternative is **the four later zones only** (Old-growth, Burn
   Scar, Riverbank, Hollow). The Meadow and Mixed Woods would keep the plain rules as the base everyone learns, and
   level 1 and daily years would be unchanged, so the daily tiers wouldn't need checking. The cost is that the two
   zones you visit most would still differ only by deck.
2. **The Old-growth.** Recommended: **the quiet** (numbers count diagonals). The alternative is the review plan's
   example, **dim light**: a turned card's number shows only once a card beside it is turned too. That fits "bring
   a light", but it takes information away, so busts rise. It's the one harsh rule in the set.
3. **The Riverbank.** Recommended: **closed in Rain, a washed-up find the month after.** The alternative is the
   review plan's example, **closed for the month after Rain** and nothing more. That's simpler, but it's a pure loss
   and plays just like Fog closing the Mixed Woods.
4. **Any rule you'd swap.** Runners-up I'd use instead:
   - Meadow: one card in every patch is dealt face up (less flavor).
   - Mixed Woods: only mushrooms of the same species show (weaker, and rare in a 9-card patch).
   - Burn Scar: morels come up in a line (hard to read, hard to build).
   - Hollow: deep finds start on the second patch (data only, but close to the creek's washed-up find).

## Balance

- **Expected:** a small rise, mostly from fewer busts in the Meadow, Mixed Woods, Burn Scar and Hollow, and a little
  from finds the fairy ring and mycelium lead to. My guess is +1 to +3 median points at most levels, with the Burn
  Scar's bust rate falling the most.
- **Measure, before and after:** `npm run sim -- 20 plan 30`, the gen sim, the baseline sim and
  `npm run sim -- 20 feast 30`. Compare tier rates and medians per level, and daily-year tiers from the baseline.
  A scratch script also reports, per zone, the bust rate, finds per trip and trips per year, so each rule's effect
  shows on its own.
- **The bar:** if any level's tier rates rise by more than about 5 points, raise `tierLift` there. If daily years
  rise by more than 5, weaken the mycelium to same-species first, then the fairy ring, rather than retune the daily
  tiers.
- **Bot:** the bots must play each rule, or the sims understate it. `safestCard` reads the same neighbours as the
  engine (diagonals in the Old-growth), treats a ringed or marked card as risk 0 and counts face-up Ash as turned. In
  the Hollow, after a turnaround the bot walks on if cards are left, or else heads home. The planner's zone scoring
  doesn't need to know about the washed-up find.

## Implementation plan

- **Data (`zones.json`):** `"rule": "fairyRing" | "mycelium" | "quiet" | "openSky" | "creek" | "turnedAround"` on each
  zone, with `"ruleName"` and `"ruleText"` for the picker. `weather.json`: Rain gains `"closes": ["riverbank"]` and its
  rule text.
- **Types:** `ZoneDef.rule`, `PatchSpot.ring` and `PatchSpot.sign` (both optional), `ForageTrip.turnedAround`,
  `GameState.washed`, the `turnedAround` draw outcome. `migrate` defaults `washed` to false.
- **Engine (`engine.ts`):**
  - `hazardsNear` takes its neighbours from a new `nearCells(trip, index, c)`. In the Old-growth on the surface,
    that's `neighbors(index, n, true)` (diagonals, a new argument in `board.ts`). The bot uses the same function.
  - `dealPatch`:
    - Fairy ring on the first surface patch: pull a random specimen from the draw pile, deal 8, and put it at the
      centre with `ring: true`.
    - Open sky: Ash cards are flipped as they're dealt, with outcome `nothing`, and go to the discard.
    - Creek: if `s.washed`, a `deepFind` goes into the first patch, then `washed` is cleared.
  - `flip`:
    - Mycelium: after a specimen, set `sign` on face-down specimen neighbours.
    - Turned around: Lost Trail in the Hollow (surface) gives outcome `turnedAround`, sets `trip.turnedAround`, and
      neither counts as a hazard nor spends the Knife. `flip` refuses while `turnedAround` is set ("The trail has
      turned you around. Walk on or head home."). `deeper` clears it.
  - `rollWeather`: set `s.washed = (old weather was rain)` before rolling the new one. The Rain closure goes through
    the existing `closes` check in `zoneStatus`, with the reason "Closed by high water".
- **Text (`text.ts`):** `outcomeText` for `turnedAround`, the first-time rule messages, the washed-up message, the
  trip log line, picker rule lines, and labels: "Face-down card, centre, in a fairy ring: a mushroom" and "Face-down
  card, a mushroom shows through".
- **UI (`Forage.tsx`, `art.tsx`, `styles.css`):** the ring and the pale cap on card backs, the mycelium threads,
  the ringed number in the Old-growth, face-up Ash dealt already turned, dimmed cards after a turnaround, the deck's
  "Walk on" prompt, and the picker rule line. The Lost Trail reveal plays as now, and the patch stays put until you
  swipe.
- **Bot (`scripts/bot.ts`):** `safestCard` uses `nearCells` and treats `ring`/`sign` as risk 0. The forage loop
  walks on or leaves after `turnedAround`.
- **Tests (`tests/zone-rules.test.ts`):**
  - The Meadow's first patch has a ringed mushroom at the centre on every trip, and the deck's contents are
    unchanged.
  - The mycelium marks exactly the face-down specimen neighbours, and nothing in another zone.
  - Old-growth numbers count diagonals on the surface but not below. The bot and the engine agree.
  - Burn Scar Ash is dealt face up, and Nothing Left Unturned still works there.
  - Rain closes the Riverbank. The month after, only the first trip gets a washed-up find.
  - Lost Trail in the Hollow doesn't bust or use the Knife, blocks flips, and allows deeper and home. On the last
    patch, only home.
  - The existing engine, underground, cache, tonight and achievement tests still pass.
- **Browser check at 375 px:** a Meadow first patch with the ring, a Mixed Woods cluster, an Old-growth ringed
  number, a Burn Scar patch with Ash up, the Riverbank closed in Rain and a washed-up find, and a Hollow turnaround.
- Then DESIGN.md, a Progress entry in `review-plan.md` with the sim numbers, and `tierLift` if the bar is crossed.
- **Build order:** one rule per commit, in the order of the table, with the bot change in the same commit. Run the
  after-sims once, at the end.
